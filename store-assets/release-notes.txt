Version 1.1.0 improves scan reliability and result recovery. A new scan or page navigation cannot mix results from a previous document. Stop cancels an active scan while retaining its partial report, Clear results removes the visible report and page overlays, and Export all CSV saves the complete current report regardless of the selected filter.

The results panel is responsive and uses clear labels for OK, Redirect, Broken, Other, and Total. Access and rate-limit responses are identified as Needs access or Rate limited under Other. Link checks use direct destination requests without credentials, telemetry, or a developer-operated processing backend.
